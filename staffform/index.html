HELPERS & MODERATORS

A little help goes a long way.

Help people find their way around Droid Archives, keep useful feedback from getting lost, and make the community a welcoming place.

01

Helper

Answer questions, point people towards tools and information, and pass useful suggestions and feedback to the team.

02

Moderator

Do everything a Helper does, plus deal with spam and inappropriate behaviour and keep discussions organised.

You do not need to know everything. We are looking for a small number of active, helpful and sensible people who care about the project.
